Coursework comparing two ways of teaching a car to drive in Unity.

- **PPO** reinforcement learning through Unity ML-Agents.
- **PilotNet** behavioural cloning trained on human driving data.
- Tracks are generated reproducibly from seeds, so runs can be compared against each other and against human drivers.
